CAPACITIVE TOUCH SAFETY SENSOR - MATERIALS LIST
================================================

MICROCONTROLLER
- Arduino Uno R3 (or Nano)

CAPACITORS
- Ceramic capacitors 10pF
- Ceramic capacitors 100pF

RESISTORS
- 1 MegaOhm (1M) resistors
- 10 KiloOhm (10K) resistors
- 220 Ohm resistors (for LED)

SENSING SURFACE
- Copper tape (adhesive) or aluminum foil

WIRING & CONNECTIONS
- Jumper wires (M-M, M-F)
- Breadboard
- USB cable (Type A to B for Arduino Uno)

FEEDBACK COMPONENTS
- LED (any color)
- Buzzer/Piezo speaker
- Push button (for calibration)

TOOLS
- Wire strippers
- Multimeter
- Scissors/knife
